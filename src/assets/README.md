# Portfolio Portrait Instructions

To display your real photograph on your portfolio:

1. **Option 1 (Recommended):**
   Save your portrait image as:
   `public/portrait.jpg`
   *(or `public/portrait.png` / `public/portrait.webp` - update the extension in `src/data/profile.js`)*

2. **Option 2:**
   Place your portrait inside this folder (`src/assets/portrait.jpg`) and update `src/data/profile.js`.

### Recommended Image Specs:
- **Aspect Ratio:** 4:5 or 3:4 (vertical / portrait orientation)
- **Resolution:** 800×1000px or higher
- **Format:** JPG, PNG, or WebP
- **Background:** Clean, solid, subtle neutral, or professional setting matching the dark charcoal theme.

The portfolio will automatically detect the image and replace the placeholder seamlessly!
